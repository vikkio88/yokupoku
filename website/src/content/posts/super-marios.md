I've mentioned a couple of time in this blog that I've got a daughter who is now almost 5, and being videogames my biggest passion, I decided to bring her down into the pit with me starting at an early age.

I have to be honest though, I am not against screentime, but I try to keep both of my kids screentime to max 1h a day or so, so videogames only take 30 minutes or so a day, just because I have seen how overstimulated she can get if we exceed that. With this out of the way let me tell you a little story.

I played [Freshly Frosted](/reviews/freshly-frosted-lovely-and-challenging) and [Hidden Folks](/reviews/hidden-folks-find-waldo-wally-on-steroids) with her, but also a few more games I did not review.

I saw that her interest fell a few sessions in, because there was not much of a long time target to reach, and that is the same with all those silly tablet games they do for her generation, I rarely let her play any of those silly tablet games, only on plane journeys really.

What does a father do when wants to find the right way? Goes back to his childhood, and a few years back I had bought myself an [Anbernic RG35XX](https://anbernic.com/en-gb/products/rg35xx) to play my **TOTALLY LEGAL** backup copies of games.

And one of the simplest game I have played in my childhood, even though I did not own a **NES**, was [Super Mario Bros.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Mario_Bros.).

So I loaded it up and let her play, she was annoyed at first but once she saw me play a few lives she got the gist of it and we got together through the first few world.

She was so happy to spend that time with me and she started to get into Mario, so much so that I decided to show her the first movie that came out few years back: [The Super Mario Bros. Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Super_Mario_Bros._Movie).

Weak plot and funny fanservice, but enjoyable. She got more into it, into the lore, the characters and I decided to try to play with her all the main Mario titles ever made.

So I will share some info and intel on what we played together, what she thought of it and what I thought of it.

## Super Mario Bros. (1985 NES)
### Her
This was her first, she loved it but it get hard quickly, too hard for a 5 years old, so I had to pick it up and do some specific sections for her.

### Me
This was the game I played at the house of my cousin, one life each and we never finished it.
It reminds me of great times and the gameplay still holds today weirdly.

## Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels (1986 FDS), Super Mario Bros. 2 (1988 NES), Super Mario Bros. 3 (1988 NES).
### Her
She found those too complex and confusing, the coulours, the complexity and the fact that they are hard from the get go.
### Me
Same really, we played them on the **Anbernic** and they were not only too hard but also weird and convoluted, they did not age well at all.

## Super Mario Land (1989 Game Boy).
### Her
She found this weird to control and not really interesting, she lost interest after 1 session.
### Me
Same thing, you can tell that is not made by the same people, it is weird and the physics is completely different. Definitely not a good game.

## Super Mario World (1990 SNES).
### Her
She loved the first appearance of [Yoshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoshi) in this. She found it really hard after the first few levels and lost interest.
### Me
I got obsessed with this, and I started playing it at night with save-states to get really far, I got to the [Chocolate Island](https://www.mariowiki.com/Chocolate_Island) bit then got bored.
But it has definitely aged very well, it is insane how good this game still is almost 40 years after release.

## Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins (1992 Game Boy).
### Her
She did not like this because of the missing colours
### Me
I did not like it very well because of the artwork, it felt a step backward from the SNES one.

## Super Mario 64 (1996 Nintendo 64).
### Her
Here we go starting with the 3d games, she did not like it at all, confusing and hard to control.

### Me
I had played this ages ago, on an emulator, but never got into it, definitely still not my favourite, has not aged very well.

## Super Mario Sunshine (2002 GameCube).
### Her
She did not try this, I know she would not like the 3d controls

### Me 
I played it on an GameCube emulator and I found the controls terrible and hard to remember, also has not aged very well.

## New Super Mario Bros. Wii (2009 Wii).
### Her
She loves it, it is definitely the one we have played the most, probably 3 hours a week every week in total, she knows it very well the first world levels and the fact that we can play side by side, throw each other stuff, help each other, and she can put herself in a bubble for the hardest section definitely makes it the best 2 player games we have ever played together.

### Me
I love this game so much, I know for sure we are creating some core memories together, I got told by my daughter a few times *"Calm down papa, we lost this time but we still have 90 lives"* which makes me feel like I thought her well to keep calm.
We usually try a few new levels, then go back to World 1 - 3 where there is Yoshi, we have done that level probably 5000 times and she never stops enjoying and neither do it.
Definitely the best game we have played together.

## New Super Mario Bros. (2006 Nintendo DS).
### Her
She played a few time, whilst on holiday, she was so happy about the mega mushroom power up, but she did not get far and she found it weird to use.
### Me
I would agree, the controls are weird but I can see where the Wii one came from, it has aged ok, but not as good as the Wii version.

## Super Mario Galaxy (2007 Wii).
### Her
She played with me a few times, not a great experience, 3d is hard already, the fact that the perspective switches whilst you go around makes her really confused.
### Me
I would say it seemed good on **Wii**, but the nunchuck controls are horrible, and the need to point the remote to gather stuff is annoying and I got so upset that I never played it again after the first two times.
I got this + 2 for the [Switch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_Switch) and I have been playing it a bit in the last few days, loving it a bit more, aged okish.

## Super Mario Galaxy 2 (2010 Wii).
### Her
She hasnt played it.
### Me
I have it on the Switch, the version [Galaxy + Galaxy 2](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-Switch-games/Super-Mario-Galaxy-Super-Mario-Galaxy-2-2915270.html) havent played this yet, will finish/get bored of Galaxy first.

## Super Mario 3D Land (2011 Nintendo 3DS).
### Her
She tried it on the **2DS** and found very confusing.
### Me
I loved this, I played all the way to world 5, then I found out I needed not to have spent the green stars to unlock a castle and lost the will of re-playing all of the old levels, but it is amazing.
It is the right mix of 2d and 3d and keeps it fun and interesting.

## New Super Mario Bros. 2 (2012 Nintendo 3DS).
### Her
She played it once, but she does not find it as fun as the **wii** version.

### Me
I have been playing it for around 15hours and I am at the last bowser castle where I keep dying.
It is by far, imho the best platformer I've played with.

## New Super Mario Bros. U (2012 Wii U).
### Her
We do not have it (YET!)
### Me
I haven't got this because I cannot find it for under £25, which is £5 more than my budget for any retro-game. There is a switch version, that is what I will be getting not the **WiiU** one, as I do not see any reason to get that console, not even now that is super cheap second hand.

## Super Mario 3D World (2013 Wii U).
### Her + Me
We played the **Switch** version of this, few rows underneath

## Super Mario Odyssey (2017 Nintendo Switch).
### Her
She did not try it.
### Me
I got it in a bundle with few other games second hand, I played only a few minutes, and I have to say it carries along loads of other 3d mario games controls which I do not know, but probably will come back to it after I finished some of the more fun ones.

##  Super Mario 3D World + Bowser’s Fury (2021 Nintendo Switch).
### Her
She loves the [cat mario powerup](https://www.mariowiki.com/Cat_Mario), as cats are one of her favourite creatures at the moment, but the game is more 3d than 3dland, so she has not been too impressed by the controls.
### Me
I am loving this, I found it the perfect amount of challenge for a few tries every now and then.
Haven't tried the [Bowser Fury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowser%27s_Fury) bit yet.

## Super Mario Bros. Wonder (2023 Nintendo Switch).
### Her
She fell in love with it, but I think she finds it overstimulating, too many things happening on screen and get her confused and lost. I would not suggest this for little kids.

### Me
I played with her and I have to say I find the same, it looks amazing, you can tell that they have watched that [Juice it or Lose it](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fy0aCDmgnxg) gamedev talk, about how games feel better if they have some **juice**.
This is just too much, the level of New Super Mario Bros. 2 was perfect for me.
I might try again in a few month.

and that is all of them.


## World 6 - 🏰 (= Conclusion)
I might start to add few of them to the [Games](/products) and do some individual [reviews](/reviews), but I have to say that getting back into those console, have them phisically in my hands, use the cartriges rather than just clicking a button to install games is something that I thought I would not miss but I did, and now I don't anymore. 
The games are all with me, I do not need a connection, a store. It is all there ready for me to plop inside the console and play.

I would add, to conclude, after having paid maybe £70 on console and £50 in games in a year or so, the best mario games for me are: **New Super Mario Bros. 2** and **Super Mario 3D Land**, it is insane how well they have aged and how good it feels to play them now, after all those years.
